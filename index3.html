About TREES

A New Voice in Open Access Publishing

Founded on the principle that scientific knowledge should grow freely and reach everyone, TREES is building a publisher that puts authors, readers, and research integrity first.

Our Mission

To nurture the global growth of science by providing a transparent, ethical, and accessible platform for researchers to share their work with the world — without barriers.

  • Open Access by Design — Every article freely available from day one.
  • Rigorous Peer Review — Quality ensured through expert evaluation.
  • Author-Centric — Fair fees, clear communication, full support.
  • Ethical Standards — Aligned with COPE, DOAJ, and OASPA principles.

Global Reach

Connecting researchers across continents with a borderless publishing platform.

Integrity First

Committed to research ethics, transparency, and academic honesty.

Fast Publication

Streamlined workflows to get your research published without delay.

Community Driven

Built with researchers, for researchers — your voice shapes our direction.

How It Works

Our Publication Process

A clear, transparent journey from submission to published research.

1
Submit

Upload your manuscript through our online portal with all required materials.

2
Review

Editorial screening followed by double-blind peer review from subject experts.

3
Revise

Authors receive detailed feedback and have the opportunity to refine their work.

4
Publish

Accepted articles are professionally typeset, assigned a DOI, and published OA.
