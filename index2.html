About TREES

A publisher built from the foundations up.

TREES is a new open access publishing company focused on technology, research, engineering, and empirical sciences.

We are creating the foundations for a publishing programme designed around rigorous scholarship, transparent editorial practices, accessibility, and responsible dissemination of research.

Rather than beginning with a catalogue of journals, TREES is beginning with a clear purpose: to build scholarly publishing platforms that researchers can trust and readers can access.

We are at the beginning of our journey — and we believe the foundations matter.

Publishing principles

What we intend to stand for.

A publishing company earns trust over time. TREES is establishing its principles before establishing its journal portfolio.

01

Open access

We believe scholarly knowledge should be accessible to readers without unnecessary barriers.

02

Editorial integrity

Editorial decisions should be grounded in scholarly quality, appropriate methodology, and transparent processes.

03

Research integrity

We will promote responsible research, ethical conduct, transparency, and reproducibility wherever appropriate.

04

Community

Authors, reviewers, editors, institutions, and readers are essential participants in the scholarly publishing ecosystem.

05

Long-term thinking

We aim to build enduring scholarly infrastructure rather than optimise for short-term publication volume.
