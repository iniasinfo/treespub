Editorial Standards & Ethics

Every TREES journal operates under a unified code of scholarly conduct — because trust is the foundation of publishing.

Read Our Policies
I

Peer Review Integrity

Double-blind review conducted by active researchers. Reviewers are selected for expertise, not affiliation, and all conflicts are disclosed.

II

Open Access by Principle

Gold open access under CC-BY licensing. All articles freely available from the moment of publication — no embargoes, no paywalls.

III

Ethical Alignment

Full adherence to COPE guidelines, DOAJ criteria, and OASPA principles. Plagiarism screening via industry-standard tools on every submission.

IV

Long-Term Preservation

Every article receives a DOI and is archived with a trusted digital repository, ensuring permanent accessibility for future scholarship.

About TREES Press

Founded in 2026, TREES Press is an independent open access publisher with a singular mission: to cultivate scholarship that is rigorous, accessible, and enduring.

We are a young press — but our principles are old ones. Editorial independence. Author respect. Reader access. Scholarly integrity. These are not innovations; they are obligations.

Our name — TREES — reflects what we believe scholarship should be: rooted in method, branching into discovery, and bearing fruit for generations of readers.

01
Independence

Free from commercial or political influence in editorial decisions.

02
Accessibility

Knowledge belongs to everyone. Open access is not optional.

03
Rigor

Peer review is sacred. We do not compromise on quality.

04
Stewardship

We preserve scholarship for the long arc of human knowledge.
