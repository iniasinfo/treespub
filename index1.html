Why TREES

Building a publisher for the future of research.

Scientific publishing is evolving. Researchers need trustworthy venues for sharing discoveries, transparent scholarly processes, and access to research that is not constrained by traditional barriers.

TREES is being established with these needs at its core. Our aim is to create a thoughtful, rigorous, and accessible publishing platform spanning technology, engineering, empirical sciences, and interdisciplinary research.

We are starting deliberately — with the foundations first, and the journals to follow.

Scope

Where ideas meet evidence.

TREES will develop publishing programmes across a broad spectrum of contemporary research.

Emerging technologies, computing, artificial intelligence, information systems, and technological innovation.

Engineering

Engineering research addressing complex systems, infrastructure, design, materials, energy, and applied innovation.

Empirical Sciences

Evidence-driven research grounded in observation, experimentation, measurement, and reproducible scientific inquiry.

Interdisciplinary Research

Research that crosses conventional boundaries to address questions that require multiple perspectives and disciplines.

Applied Research

Emerging Fields

New and evolving areas of inquiry where evidence, innovation, and interdisciplinary thinking can open new directions.

Our principles

Publishing with purpose.

Our approach starts with the values we believe should underpin modern scholarly communication.

01

Open by design

We believe research should be discoverable and accessible to readers wherever they are.

02

Rigour before volume

Our focus is on building trusted scholarly venues, rather than pursuing publication volume for its own sake.

03

Researchers at the centre

We want authors, reviewers, editors, and readers to have a meaningful voice in the publishing ecosystem.

04

Responsible scholarship

Integrity, transparency, reproducibility, and ethical research practices should remain fundamental to scholarly publishing.
